# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Members, contributors, and leaders pledge to keep this community free of harassment
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior:

- Show empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and apologize to those affected.
- Consider the community's interests.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainer clarifies and enforces these standards and takes fair corrective action
for inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful behavior.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to [@f-bader](https://github.com/f-bader) through GitHub.
The maintainer reviews and investigates all complaints promptly and fairly.
